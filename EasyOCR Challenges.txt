| #      | Challenge                    | Risk Threshold / Condition                         | Impact                                                |
| ------ | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**  | **Very small text**          | **< 8 px character height**                        | URL characters may be missed or misread               |
| **2**  | **Low-resolution PDF/scan**  | **< 150 DPI**                                      | Significant reduction in OCR accuracy                 |
| **3**  | **Blurred text**             | Visible blur / unclear character edges             | `O/0`, `I/l/1` and similar characters can be confused |
| **4**  | **Special characters**       | URLs containing `? & = % _ - #`                    | Characters may be missed or incorrectly recognized    |
| **5**  | **Long URLs**                | **>150–200 characters**                            | URL may be truncated or incorrectly reconstructed     |
| **6**  | **URL split across lines**   | URL contains line breaks                           | OCR may return separate text segments                 |
| **7**  | **Low contrast**             | Poor text/background contrast                      | URL may not be detected                               |
| **8**  | **Multiple URLs on a page**  | **5+ URLs/page**                                   | URLs can be missed, merged, or incorrectly ordered    |
| **9**  | **Rotated/skewed text**      | Rotation **>15–20°** / skew **>5–10°**             | Detection accuracy can decrease                       |
| **10** | **Complex background/noise** | Text over images, patterns, stamps, or noisy scans | False detections and incorrect characters             |

🔴 Highest priority for your POC

If you only want to test 5 scenarios, test:

<8 px text → <150 DPI → special characters → long URLs → URLs split across lines
