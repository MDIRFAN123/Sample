# AI Campaign Copilot for Adobe Workfront

## Vision

Enhance Adobe Workfront with an AI-powered Campaign Copilot that helps users create complete, accurate, and high-quality campaign requests by providing intelligent, real-time guidance based on historical campaign data, reviewer feedback, and business best practices.

---

# Business Challenge

Adobe Workfront provides the required fields and instructions for campaign creation. However, users still spend significant time preparing campaign requests, and reviewers often request additional clarifications due to missing, inconsistent, or unclear information.

This results in:

* Multiple review and approval cycles.
* Increased manual effort for campaign creators and reviewers.
* Inconsistent campaign documentation across teams.
* Longer campaign turnaround times.
* Valuable knowledge remaining scattered across historical campaigns instead of being reused.

---

# Proposed Solution

Embed an AI-powered Campaign Copilot directly within Adobe Workfront to assist users while they create campaign requests.

Rather than replacing the existing process, the AI will enhance it by providing contextual recommendations, validating campaign information, and leveraging organizational knowledge from historical campaigns.

The Campaign Copilot will act as a real-time assistant, helping users submit higher-quality campaign requests on the first attempt.

---

# Key Capabilities

### Intelligent Content Recommendations

Suggest improved offer descriptions, campaign objectives, and business details based on similar approved campaigns.

### Smart Auto-Completion

Automatically recommend commonly used information for similar campaign types, reducing manual effort.

### Historical Campaign Intelligence

Recommend similar historical campaigns so users can reuse proven approaches instead of creating requests from scratch.

### Missing Information Detection

Identify mandatory information that has not been provided before the campaign is submitted.

### Cross-Field Validation

Validate consistency across campaign fields, including offer type, campaign dates, eligibility, rewards, and campaign details.

### Reviewer Feedback Prediction

Learn from historical reviewer comments and proactively recommend improvements before submission.

### Campaign Readiness Score

Provide an overall readiness score with actionable recommendations, helping users resolve issues before sending the request for review.

### Business Rule & Best Practice Validation

Ensure campaign requests follow standard business guidelines, approved terminology, and documentation best practices.

### Continuous Learning

Continuously improve recommendations by learning from newly approved campaigns and reviewer feedback.

# Human-in-the-Loop

The AI will assist users by providing recommendations and insights. Final decisions will always remain with the campaign creator and reviewer, ensuring appropriate business judgment and oversight throughout the process.

---

# Security & Governance

The solution will operate within the organization's existing security and governance framework. Historical campaign data will be used responsibly, with appropriate access controls and compliance with internal data protection policies.

---

# Implementation Roadmap

### Phase 1 – Foundation

### Phase 2 – Intelligent Validation

* Campaign readiness score.
* Cross-field validation.

### Phase 3 – AI Campaign Intelligence

* Knowledge search across historical campaigns.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Our long-term vision is to transform Adobe Workfront into an AI-powered Campaign Intelligence platform that not only manages campaign workflows but also captures organizational knowledge, guides users with intelligent recommendations, improves campaign quality, and continuously enhances the campaign creation and review experience.

By embedding AI directly into the existing workflow, we can reduce operational inefficiencies, improve collaboration, and enable teams to deliver campaigns faster with greater consistency and confidence.
