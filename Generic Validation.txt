# Generic Worksheet Validation

Validate worksheet "{sheet_name}" using general workbook validation controls.

- Review worksheet structure, headers, ranges, and data organization.
- Identify missing, duplicate, malformed, or contradictory required values.
- Validate formulas, references, data types, dates, and number formats.
- Check internal consistency and evident worksheet business rules.
- Compare shared identifiers and totals with related worksheets where applicable.

Report every issue with its row or cell reference, severity, evidence, and recommended remediation.
