[Waterfall]
- Confirm every allocation tier is evaluated in the correct sequence.
- Verify percentage allocations total 100% for each applicable period.
- Flag broken formulas, circular references, and unexplained negative balances.
- Reconcile the final distributed amount with the source total.
- Report each issue with its cell reference, severity, and recommended correction.

[QA Report]
- Confirm every required QA check has a result and supporting evidence.
- Flag checks marked Passed when the actual value differs from the expected value.
- Identify duplicate checks, missing owners, and invalid completion dates.
- Treat blank or unknown results as incomplete validation.
- Summarize failures by severity and include the affected row or cell reference.
